6. NCCL sweep: best config vs KEEP (details: nccl-sweep.json, nccl-graph-diag.txt)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Exact decode sizes: AR 10/30/40/60/80 KiB (m 1/3/4/6/8), logits AG 379/505/758/1010 KiB per rank.
NCCL 2.30.7, single rail RoCE, GDR off (libmlx5 50.0 lacks mlx5dv_reg_dmabuf_mr).
Clean runs, gapped steady cost per call (us, KEEP vs best):
  AR m=3 58.8 -> 21.5, m=4 57.6 -> 22.5, m=8 63.2 -> 29.1, logits AG m=4 100.9 -> 61.4; eager AR m=4 27.7 -> 25.7.
Modeled collective critical path per step: KEEP 6.19 ms (c=1) / 6.43 ms (c=2);
NCCL_GRAPH_MIXING_SUPPORT=0: 3.10 / 3.37 ms -> -3.08 / -3.06 ms/step. Every run of graph_mixing0
(1 clean + 2 contaminated) reads 22.5-23.8 us at AR m=4; KEEP reads 54.9-62.8.
Mechanism (profiled diagnostics, clean): with mixing on, each captured network collective slows the
neighbouring bandwidth-bound kernel by 13.5% median (32% in the first slot) and adds 3 us of launch
gap; mixing off removes both. Graph startup (300-800 us per replay) is unchanged.
All other knobs lose to or tie KEEP in the clean runs (c=1 modeled delta): PROTO=LL +0.46 (logits AG
slower), Simple +1.03, LL128 +1.67, ALGO Ring +0.18, NCHANNELS 1 +0.58 / 2 -0.19 / 4 -0.15 / MIN 8 -0.14
(all worse at c=2), NTHREADS 128/256 +0.19/+0.05, bare (no KEEP) +0.23; IB_USE_INLINE, PROXY_CPUSET big
or little, IGNORE_CPU_AFFINITY, NET_GDR_C2C, NTHREADS 64/512, allreduce:tree have contaminated reps only
and none beat KEEP there either. A global NCCL_ALGO=Tree is invalid (AllGather has no Tree).
Safety: NCCL docs, NCCL_GRAPH_MIXING_SUPPORT=0 does not support "launching a non-captured NCCL collective
during an outstanding graph launch that uses the same communicator, regardless of stream ordering". The
trace shows the eager logits AG launched 45.4 ms (median; min 39.9) and the eager embed AR 32.2 ms before
the target graph ends, in 100% of steps. So the env var alone is unsafe here. Safe design: route
non-captured TP collectives to a second PyNcclCommunicator (or ncclConfig_t graphUsageMode per
communicator), then A/B in the serve.
