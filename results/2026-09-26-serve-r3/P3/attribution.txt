8. Caveats
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    - One profiled window per arm and concurrency. Kernel medians rest on 98-105 c=1 and 30-34 c=2 steps x
    40 layers; step-level numbers are single-window readings, not boot-to-boot noise-gated values.
    - Profiled step walls (A 63.47/63.45, K 49.15/49.31 ms at c=1) sit 0.3-1.4 ms above the same boots'
    unprofiled L.A.I.L x3 (A 62.11, K 48.84 ms/step). Host spans (the Engram stage) are inflated the most.
    - K_p worker traces (all 4) had NUL bytes at exactly the 4096-byte-page-final byte after every newline
    (4705 / 4278 / 3739 / 2853), JSON indentation only. A_p traces and the K_p API-server trace had none.
    They were parsed after a lossless NUL->space rewrite (*.nul2space.pt.trace.json.gz next to the
    originals). The cause is in K_p's worker processes and is not identified.
    - c=2 windows are 2 x 80 tokens (rule fixed before the window: MemAvailable after the c=1 window was 11
    GiB). The tail steps after the shorter stream ended (m=4) are excluded.
    - Projections are from isolated microbenches (the workstreams' unprofiled numbers). A lever's kernel can
    hit its projection while its step gain is masked or amplified by what runs next to it; both are
    reported.
